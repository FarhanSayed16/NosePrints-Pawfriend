# Staff SOP — how to capture, confirm, and what never to share

For PawFriend volunteers using NosePrints during a closed pilot.

## Capture (register or identify)

1. Hold the phone **15–30 cm** from the nose.
2. Keep the **nose in the circle with a bit of muzzle showing**. Do not fill the whole frame with only the nose — the detector needs an object to find.
3. Use **daylight or even indoor light**. Avoid heavy shadow on one side of the nose.
4. Hold still. If the app rejects the photo, retake — do not upload a blurry crop.
5. Register **3–5 nose photos** per dog when possible (different angles).

## Identify

1. Anyone can scan. The public result shows **dog name, breed, color, photos, and a similarity score**.
2. **Owner phone, email, and address are never shown on Identify.**
3. **Likely match** (score ≥ 0.56) and **possible match** (0.51–0.56) both go to the staff queue. Possible matches need extra caution.
4. **No match:** use **List as found**. That is not owner registration. Finder phone is optional and staff-only.

## Confirm a match (`/staff`)

1. Log in with your PawFriend staff account.
2. Open the queue item. Compare **this scan** vs the **stored nose photo** side by side.
3. Confirm only if you believe it is the same dog. If unsure, reject or ask a second staff member.
4. After confirm, owner contact appears **on this screen only**. Call or email the owner yourself. The app may also email the owner if SMTP is configured.
5. If the dog was marked lost, confirm sets status back to **registered**.

## Report lost

Only staff can mark a dog lost (Directory → Report lost after login, or `/report-lost/:id`). Add a last-seen note (area, collar, time).

## Never share

- Owner phone / email / address in WhatsApp groups, public posts, or the Identify screen
- Finder phone from a found listing
- Screenshots of the staff confirm panel
- Claims of **99% accuracy** or “the app proved this is their dog”

## Honest numbers (lab, not street)

Held-out gallery-probe on the CVPR 2022-style set: **rank-1 78.6%**, **AUC 0.80**. Street phones will be worse. You are the decision-maker.
